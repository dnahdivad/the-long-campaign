How it plays

  • Everyone writes orders in secret. When the deadline comes — or as soon as every general has sent orders — the month is fought all at once.
  • Take towns for recruits, hold farms and railways for supply, and raise infantry, cavalry, artillery, engineers and a general.
  • Make deals on WhatsApp. On the map you can support another army's attack, share your scouts' reports, grant free passage or send supplies — and take any of it back next month.
  • Each general has secret orders worth points at the end. Nobody knows who is really winning.
  • Every map is new and fair: the board is built in identical slices, one per army, with the richest ground in the middle.
  • Weather fronts cross the map: rain floods the fords, fog blinds the scouts, snow starves armies out of supply.

Empty seats are taken by computer generals, so you can play two humans against four, or one against the lot.
